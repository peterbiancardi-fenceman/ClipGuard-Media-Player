BLACKMAGIC DESIGN DECKLINK SDK -- NOTICE AND LICENCE STATEMENT

ClipGuard Media Player contains code compiled against headers from the Blackmagic
Design DeckLink SDK:

  decklink_monitor.dll          Read-only signal status monitor, built from
                                decklink_monitor.c against the SDK's DeckLinkAPI.h
                                and DeckLinkAPI_i.c.
  filters\DeckLinkSource.ax     DirectShow capture source filter.

Both come from Unplugged's engine (github.com/spedagadi/unplugged-mpc-hc), in our
fork with our changes: github.com/peterbiancardi-fenceman/unplugged-mpc-hc
(branch clipguard-media-player).
