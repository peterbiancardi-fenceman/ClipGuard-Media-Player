ClipGuard Media Player (beta)

Please read before installing.

VIDEO PROCESSOR AND COPY PROTECTION
Video Processor needs an unencrypted HDMI signal. ClipGuard Media Player does not
decrypt, remove or bypass HDCP or any other copy protection. It cannot record or
save the video it shows; it only displays it. You are responsible for using it
with sources and content you are permitted to use, and in line with the laws
where you live and the terms of your devices and services.

BETA
This is a beta. Report problems as issues on
https://github.com/peterbiancardi-fenceman/ClipGuard-Media-Player/issues

PRIVACY
No telemetry. Your Plex sign-in, settings and logs stay on this PC.
